Molecular Virology and Cancer Biology

The MVCB laboratory at Gauhati University studies the molecular basis of infectious and non-infectious diseases, including viral hepatitis and cancer, in the north-east Indian population.

Explore our researchContact the lab

Research

Our lab aims to delineate the mechanisms behind the pathogenesis of infectious and non-infectious diseases that cause morbidity and mortality in the north-east Indian population, using molecular biology tools.

Virology and immunology

Molecular virology, especially viral hepatitis, and molecular immunology.

Reproductive biology

Problems such as preterm delivery and recurrent pregnancy loss.

Cancer biology

Cancer biology of various organ aetiologies.

Disease biology

Rheumatoid arthritis, gastrointestinal and liver diseases.

Facilities

  • Molecular biology tools
  • Microscopy
  • Mammalian cell culture
  • Karyotyping
  • Microbiology
